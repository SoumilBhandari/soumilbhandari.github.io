How it works

Three steps, one evening.

1

Tell us who you are

Name, title, links, and your resume. One short form — no account to create, no app to download.

2

Your page goes live

A fast, mobile-first card at your own address, over HTTPS, pointed at a domain you own if you have one. Change your title later and it updates everywhere at once — including on every card you have already handed out.

3

Hand someone the card

They tap it, or scan the code on the back, and your page opens. Nothing to install on their side. The chip holds the link and never the data, which is why a new job never costs you a reprint.

The physical half

Artwork that a printer will take.

Every file is sized to CR80 — 85.5 × 54 mm, the same as a credit card — with text kept 4 mm inside the trim so nothing important dies at the cut.

  • Trim version for UV printing onto blank NFC cards
  • Bleed version with crop marks outside the bleed, where a printer can see them
  • Engraving artwork for wood and bamboo, sized for a laser's bleed
  • Live vector text and QR — no rasterised type anywhere
  • QR decoded back out of a 300 dpi render, not assumed to work
